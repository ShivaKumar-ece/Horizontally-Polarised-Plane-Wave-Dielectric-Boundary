3Space and time dependence (total field)

Space: fields along the normal (x = 0) at the current instant t. Thin faint lines show the peak envelope.
Time: fields at the probe point over two periods. The black line marks the current t.

Tangential components (Ex, Hy) are continuous across z = 0. The normal component Ez jumps, because ε1Ez1 = ε2Ez2. In medium 1 the incident and reflected waves interfere, giving a partial standing wave (the envelope ripples). Under TIR the field in medium 2 decays exponentially.
